About

Cryptographic analysis of neural network extraction.

I am a doctoral student in the School of Cyber Science and Technology at Shandong University. Before starting my Ph.D., I completed my undergraduate study at the School of Mathematics, Shandong University, from 2021 to 2025.

My current work studies neural network parameter extraction from a cryptanalytic perspective. I am particularly interested in how activation functions affect identifiability, query design, numerical robustness, and the boundary between feasible and infeasible extraction.

中文简介：山东大学网络空间安全学院一年级博士研究生，导师为王美琴教授。本科毕业于山东大学数学学院，研究方向为 Crypto for AI 与不同激活函数上的神经网络参数提取攻击。

Research

Research Interests

01

Neural Network Parameter Extraction

Recovering or characterizing hidden neural network parameters from observable input-output behavior, with explicit attention to query access, threat models, and success metrics.

02

Activation-Specific Cryptanalysis

Understanding how activation functions change attack equations, algebraic structure, numerical stability, and the complexity of cryptanalytic extraction.

03

Crypto for AI

Applying cryptographic modeling to AI security problems, including leakage assumptions, security notions, reproducible experiments, and defensible claims.
